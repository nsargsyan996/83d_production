# 83D Production

**Custom 3D printing in Yerevan** · [83d.am](https://83d.am)

We print personal gifts, branded souvenirs and decor from your idea, a sketch or a 3D file.
Orders start at a single piece, and we confirm the price and the date before anything is printed.

## What we print

- **Personal gifts:** name keychains, figurines and keepsakes, in the colours you pick
- **Branded souvenirs:** your logo in relief on keychains, stands and desk pieces, from a few for the team to a small series
- **Decor for home and office:** vases, organizers and stands, sized to the shelf they stand on
- **Your own 3D model:** send an STL, 3MF or STEP file, or a sketch, and we check that it can be printed

## How an order works

1. **Send your idea, a sketch or a 3D file.** A description with sizes or a photo is enough to start; printing needs a 3D model, and we tell you if we can make one.
2. **Agree on the model, colour and price.** The material and the date are confirmed too, before anything is printed.
3. **We print it layer by layer** and check every layer before it leaves the studio.
4. **Collect it or have it delivered.** The way and cost of delivery are agreed with the order.
